# Brabus Intelligence — Human Visual UAT (IA-V2-1)

Everything mechanical is already checked by automation (128/128 visual
QA checks, screenshots at `tests/screenshots/ia-v2-1-visual-qa/`,
contact sheet at `tests/screenshots/ia-v2-1-visual-qa/REVIEW-INDEX.html`).
This checklist is only for what a human eye judges — **please don't
re-check the math, the dates, or the percentages; automation already
proved those are correct.**

Open the review index in a browser and look at the screenshots (or run
the module locally at `#/brabus-intelligence`), then answer:

1. **Overall professionalism** — does this look like a real, finished
   part of Portal F&I, or like an unfinished prototype/demo? (The
   "DADOS DE TESTE" banner is expected — ignore that, judge everything
   else around it.)

2. **Fit with Portal V2** — sitting next to Análise Geral, Score, or
   the Simuladores, does Intelligence feel like the same product, or
   does something about it feel out of place?

3. **Conversation readability** — is the back-and-forth between you
   and the assistant easy to follow at a glance? Anything cramped,
   anything with too much empty space?

4. **Financial block hierarchy** — when a result comes back (Linear,
   Cash Conversion, Antecipação, etc.), is it obvious what the
   headline number is versus supporting detail?

5. **Desktop density (1366×768)** — screenshots 02–19. Does the screen
   feel usefully full, or is there wasted space above the fold?

6. **Mobile density (390px)** — screenshots 22–28. Does it still feel
   like a usable tool, or like a stack of oversized cards you have to
   scroll forever to get through?

7. **Fixture disclosure clarity** — is it obvious, without reading
   fine print, that this isn't talking to a real backend yet?

8. **Composer usability** — does the input area at the bottom feel
   natural to type into and send from, on both desktop and mobile?

9. **Error-state clarity** — screenshots 14–16 (401/403/upstream
   error). Do these read as calm and understandable, or alarming/
   confusing?

10. **Anything excessive or confusing** — is there anything on the
    page that feels unnecessary, decorative without purpose, or
    unclear about what it's for?

---

Record your answer per item (a sentence or two is enough) and any
screenshot numbers you're referencing. There's no pass/fail threshold
here — your judgment is the gate.
